Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on my phone or tablet?

No. VuePoint is a bookmarklet, and it needs a bookmarks bar to run from. That means a laptop or desktop.

Button opens a page instead of adding a bookmark

You clicked instead of dragged. Click-and-hold, drag to the Bookmarks Bar, release.

School blocked drag-and-drop?

Use Copy link → right-click Bookmarks Bar → Add page… → paste as URL.

I don't see the Bookmarks Bar

Try ⋮ Menu → Bookmarks → Show bookmarks bar.

Will this change my real grade?

Never. VuePoint edits only the copy on your screen. Nothing is sent back to StudentVUE.

Weighted or total: which do I use?

VuePoint detects it for you. If it guesses wrong, switch it yourself. Both percentages are shown.

Does it need internet?

No. The bookmarklet is self-contained and makes no network requests. It works offline once the gradebook has loaded.

Teacher or IT admin? VuePoint reads the gradebook already open in your window, the same rows your browser rendered, and works out the scores and category weights from them locally. Nothing is sent anywhere.
